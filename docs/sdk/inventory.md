# SDK phase 0: the inventory of src/

What each area of `src/` is actually used for by the others: its de facto public surface. Generated by `tools/sdk/inventory.py` and `tools/sdk/report.py` (`python tools/sdk/inventory.py src work/sdk-inventory/facts.json && python tools/sdk/report.py`) from the `#include` lines of every source file (third_party, testdata and tests folders left out). The design this feeds is [SDK_DESIGN.md](../SDK_DESIGN.md); phase 1 turns it into one public face per area.

27 areas, 7,716 files, 2,479,292 lines; 4,965 includes cross an area boundary in product code, 2,659 more in tests.

## Summary

- **Cycles:** 2 group(s) of areas that include each other, directly or around a loop: analyze, base, build, compare, decompile, dotnet, emulate, engine, exact, formats, invert, java, jobs, knowledge, platforms, recompile, source, store, symbols, translate, verify, xna; app, ui.
- **Upward includes** (a lower layer reaching into a higher one, against the layering in SDK_DESIGN.md): 34 area pairs, 116 includes.
- **Widely shared headers** (included from 3 or more other areas): 119.
- **Messiest public faces** (most distinct headers used from outside, then most using areas):
  - `formats`: 98 headers used from 20 other areas (422 includes)
  - `source`: 69 headers used from 6 other areas (80 includes)
  - `platforms`: 50 headers used from 14 other areas (100 includes)
  - `emulate`: 36 headers used from 4 other areas (98 includes)
  - `decompile`: 33 headers used from 17 other areas (169 includes)
  - `core`: 30 headers used from 26 other areas (3,277 includes)
  - `build`: 28 headers used from 9 other areas (90 includes)
  - `java`: 25 headers used from 9 other areas (42 includes)
